NUST Administration Portal

Course Registration

Course registration for the next semester opens two weeks before the semester begins.

Steps to Register Courses:

  1. Log in to the NUST Student Portal using your ID and password
  2. Navigate to Academic menu
  3. Click on "Course Registration"
  4. Select courses from the available list based on your program requirements
  5. Ensure you meet all prerequisites for selected courses
  6. Submit your registration before the deadline
  7. Download and print your course schedule

Late registration closes 3 days after semester starts. A late fee of Rs. 500 applies for registrations after the deadline.

Fee Submission

NUST charges semester fees for tuition, labs, and facilities. Fee payment is required before attending classes.

Fee Payment Process:

  1. Log in to your Student Portal
  2. Go to Finance section
  3. Download the tuition fee challan
  4. Pay the challan at any state bank branch or online through NUST portal
  5. Enter the transaction receipt number in the portal
  6. Verify your fee payment status online

Fee payment deadline is one week after semester starts. Late payment may result in course cancellation.

Semester fee is approximately Rs. 150,000 for BS programs and Rs. 200,000 for graduate programs.

Exam Schedules

Exam schedules are released four weeks before the exam period. Midterm exams usually occur in week 8 of the semester.

Final exams are held at the end of each semester. Exam date, time, and location are posted in the Student Portal.

Students must arrive 15 minutes before exam start time. No students allowed inside exam hall after 15 minutes.

Hostel Services

NUST provides hostel accommodation for both male and female students on campus.

Hostel Application Process:

  1. Hostel applications open in June for the academic year
  2. Submit your application through the Student Portal
  3. Provide proof of residence (school leaving certificate showing hometown)
  4. Priority given to students from remote areas
  5. Hostel allotment results announced by July 31st

Hostel charges: Rs. 30,000 per semester for shared rooms, Rs. 40,000 for single rooms.

Hostel facilities include: common rooms, study areas, sports facilities, medical clinic, and 24/7 security.

Attendance Policy

Minimum attendance requirement is 75% for each course to be eligible to sit in the final exam.

If your attendance falls below 75%, you will be marked absent (X grade) for that course.

To request attendance waiver, students must submit a formal application with valid reasons (medical or emergency) to the Academic Affairs office.

Transcript and Grade Reports

Grade reports are available in the Student Portal within two weeks after exam results are announced.

Official transcripts can be requested from the Registrar office. Processing time is 5-7 working days.

GPA calculation: NUST uses a 4.0 scale. Grade A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B- = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, F = 0.0

Graduation Requirements

Students must complete all required courses with a minimum GPA of 2.0 to be eligible for graduation.

Final semester students should apply for graduation through the Student Portal.

Graduation ceremony is held twice a year: June and December.

Financial Aid and Scholarships

NUST offers merit-based scholarships to high-achieving students. Scholarship amounts vary from 25% to 100% of tuition fee.

Scholarship applications are reviewed after first semester results.

Need-based financial aid is available through the Student Financial Aid office.
